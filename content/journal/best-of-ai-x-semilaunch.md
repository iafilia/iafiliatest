We started [Best of AI](https://bestofai.io) to make finding good AI tools less of a slog. For a long time that meant us hunting around, trying things, and deciding what made the cut. It works, but it also means the directory only reflects what we happened to find and what we happen to like.

So we're teaming up with [SemiLaunch](https://semilaunch.com), a launch platform where makers post their products and compete in weekly rankings decided by community votes.

## Why we're doing this

New AI tools show up every day, and plenty of good ones disappear without anyone noticing. We can't catch them all. The people actually using these tools often can.

SemiLaunch gives us a way to listen. Their community votes, and we get a second signal on top of our own curation.

## How it works

If you build an AI tool, here's the path to a listing on Best of AI:

1. Launch it on SemiLaunch under the AI/ML category.
2. Get people to try it, vote, and tell you what they think.
3. Finish in the weekly top 3, and an eligible AI tool gets a free listing on Best of AI.

You don't need to fill out a separate submission form, and we don't charge anything for the listing. SemiLaunch's regular launch pricing still applies, though.

You can still use our [submit form](/submit/) or send a PR on [GitHub](https://github.com/best-of-ai/best-of-ai) if you'd rather go that way. Tools that come through SemiLaunch get higher priority, though.

## What we hope happens

More exposure for makers is the obvious win. The part we like more is that real users get a say. If enough people back a tool, we'd like to know about it, and we'd like to list it.

If you've got something to show, [launch it on SemiLaunch](https://semilaunch.com/launch/bestofai).
